# Uplight vs Better Uptime: Feature Comparison

Better Uptime (now part of Better Stack) is a polished monitoring and incident management platform. Here's how it compares to Uplight's open source approach.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Uplight | Better Uptime |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Open Source | Yes | No |
| Self-Hosting | Yes | No |
| Starting Price | Free | $24/month |
| Check Intervals | 1 minute | 30 seconds |
| Regions | 9+ | 6 |
| Status Pages | Unlimited | Limited by plan |
| On-call Scheduling | No | Yes |
| Incident Management | Basic | Advanced |

## Where Better Uptime Excels

Better Uptime is a full incident management platform:

- **On-call scheduling** - Rotation schedules, escalation policies
- **Incident timelines** - Detailed incident tracking
- **Team coordination** - Acknowledge, escalate, resolve workflow
- **Beautiful UI** - Polished, modern interface
- **30-second checks** - Faster than Uplight's 1-minute minimum

## Where Uplight Wins

### 1. It's Open Source

You can:
- Read every line of code
- Self-host on your infrastructure
- Fork and modify for your needs
- Contribute improvements

Better Uptime is closed source. You trust them completely.

### 2. Self-Hosting Option

Deploy Uplight to:
- Cloudflare Workers (one-click)
- Your own servers
- Any cloud provider

Better Uptime? Their infrastructure only.

### 3. Pricing Transparency

**Better Uptime pricing:**
- **Free**: Very limited (for trying)
- **Freelancer**: $24/month
- **Small Team**: $76/month
- **Business**: $188/month
- **Enterprise**: Custom

**Uplight pricing:**
- **Self-hosted**: Free forever
- **Managed**: Free tier + affordable paid plans

### 4. No Vendor Lock-in

With Uplight, you own:
- Your monitoring configuration
- Your historical data
- Your status page setup

Leave anytime. Take everything with you.

## When to Choose Each

### Choose Uplight If:

- You want to self-host
- Open source matters to you
- You need simple, reliable monitoring
- Budget is a concern
- You have existing incident management tools
- Vendor independence is important

### Choose Better Uptime If:

- You need full incident management
- On-call scheduling is essential
- You want phone/SMS alerts
- 30-second intervals matter
- You prefer managed-only solutions
- Budget isn't a primary concern

## The Trade-offs

**Uplight trades:**
- Advanced incident management
- On-call scheduling
- Phone/SMS alerts
- 30-second intervals

**For:**
- Open source transparency
- Self-hosting freedom
- Lower (or zero) cost
- Vendor independence
- Data ownership

**Better Uptime trades:**
- Open source benefits
- Self-hosting option
- Lower cost
- Vendor independence

**For:**
- Advanced incident workflows
- On-call management
- Polished UI
- Faster check intervals
- Phone call alerts

## Migration Considerations

Moving from Better Uptime to Uplight:

**What transfers easily:**
- Monitor configurations
- Status page setup
- Basic alert integrations

**What you'll need to replace:**
- On-call scheduling (use PagerDuty, Opsgenie)
- Phone/SMS alerts (use third-party services)
- Advanced incident timelines

## Conclusion

Better Uptime is an excellent product for teams that need full incident management. It's worth the cost if you'll use those features.

Uplight is for teams who want:
- Control over their monitoring stack
- No ongoing costs (self-hosted)
- Open source transparency
- Simple, reliable uptime monitoring

Different tools for different needs. Both are valid choices.
