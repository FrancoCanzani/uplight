# Getting Started

Get up and running with Uplight in just a few minutes. This guide will walk you through creating your account, setting up your first monitor, and configuring notifications.

## Create Your Account

1. Visit [uplight.dev](https://uplight.dev) and click **Sign Up**
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox
4. You're in! You'll be taken to your dashboard

## Create Your First Team

When you sign up, a personal team is created for you automatically. You can use this for personal projects or create a new team for your organization.

To create a new team:

1. Click on the team selector in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Team**
3. Enter a name for your team
4. Click **Create**

## Add Your First Monitor

Now let's monitor something. We'll create an HTTP monitor to check if a website is up.

1. From your dashboard, click **Add Monitor**
2. Choose **HTTP** as the monitor type
3. Enter the URL you want to monitor (e.g., `https://example.com`)
4. Give your monitor a name (e.g., "Main Website")
5. Select the check interval (how often to check)
6. Choose at least one monitoring location
7. Click **Create Monitor**

Your monitor is now active and will start checking your URL immediately.

## Configure Notifications

Don't miss an outage. Set up notifications to get alerted when something goes wrong.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Notifications**
2. Click **Add Channel**
3. Choose your preferred channel (Email, Slack, Discord, etc.)
4. Follow the setup instructions for that channel
5. Click **Save**

Now you'll be notified whenever a monitor goes down or comes back up.
