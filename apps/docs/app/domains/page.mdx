# Domains & SSL Monitoring

Never be caught off guard by an expiring domain or SSL certificate. Uplight tracks expiration dates and alerts you before they become problems.

## What's Monitored

For each domain, Uplight tracks:

### Domain Information
- **Registration date** - When the domain was first registered
- **Last updated** - When domain records were last changed
- **Expiration date** - When the domain registration expires
- **Registrar** - Where the domain is registered

### SSL Certificate
- **Issuer** - Certificate authority that issued the cert
- **Valid from** - When the certificate became valid
- **Expiration date** - When the certificate expires
- **Self-signed status** - Whether it's a self-signed certificate

## How It Works

When you add a monitor for an HTTP endpoint, Uplight automatically:

1. Checks the domain WHOIS information
2. Inspects the SSL certificate
3. Records expiration dates
4. Alerts you as expirations approach

Domain and SSL checks run twice daily to keep information current.

## Viewing Domain Information

1. Navigate to **Monitors**
2. Select an HTTP monitor
3. Click on the **Domain** or **SSL** tab

You'll see current information and days until expiration.

## Expiration Alerts

Uplight alerts you at these intervals before expiration:

| Days Before | Alert Level |
|-------------|-------------|
| 30 days | Warning |
| 14 days | Warning |
| 7 days | Urgent |
| 1 day | Critical |

Alerts are sent through your configured notification channels.

## SSL Certificate Details

### Understanding Certificate Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Valid** | Certificate is current and trusted |
| **Expiring Soon** | Certificate expires within 30 days |
| **Expired** | Certificate has expired (site will show errors) |
| **Self-Signed** | Not issued by a trusted CA (browsers will warn) |

### Self-Signed Certificates

Uplight flags self-signed certificates because:
- Browsers show security warnings to visitors
- They may indicate a misconfiguration
- Let's Encrypt makes free trusted certs easy to obtain

If you intentionally use self-signed certs (internal tools, development), you can acknowledge this in the settings.

## Domain Expiration

### Why It Matters

An expired domain means:
- Your website goes offline
- Email stops working
- Someone else could register your domain
- Loss of brand and SEO value

### Best Practices

1. **Enable auto-renewal** - Most registrars offer this
2. **Keep payment info current** - Failed renewals cause expiration
3. **Use multiple contacts** - Ensure renewal notices reach someone
4. **Register for multiple years** - Reduces renewal frequency

## Common Issues

### "Domain information not available"

This can happen when:
- The domain uses privacy protection (WHOIS guard)
- The TLD doesn't support WHOIS queries
- The registrar blocks automated queries

The SSL certificate information should still be available.

### "SSL certificate shows wrong domain"

This could indicate:
- A CDN or proxy in front of your site
- Misconfigured certificate
- Using a shared hosting certificate

Check your hosting configuration if the cert doesn't match your domain.

## What Uplight Doesn't Do

- **Domain registration** - Use your registrar to manage registration
- **SSL certificate installation** - Use your hosting provider or cert manager
- **Auto-renewal** - Set this up with your registrar/provider

Uplight monitors and alerts, but you need to take action to renew.
