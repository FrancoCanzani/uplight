# What is Uplight?

Uplight is an uptime monitoring and incident management platform that helps you keep your services running smoothly. Monitor your websites, APIs, and infrastructure from multiple locations around the world, and get notified instantly when something goes wrong.

## Key Features

### Uptime Monitoring
Monitor HTTP endpoints and TCP ports from 9 global locations. Get alerted when your services go down or respond slowly.

### Heartbeat Monitoring
For cron jobs, scheduled tasks, and background processes that should run on a schedule. If Uplight doesn't receive a ping in time, you'll be notified.

### Incident Management
Track, manage, and resolve incidents with a complete timeline. Assign team members, set severity levels, and document post-mortems.

### Domain & SSL Monitoring
Never let your domains or SSL certificates expire unexpectedly. Uplight tracks expiration dates and warns you in advance.

### Multi-Channel Notifications
Get alerted where you work: Email, Slack, Discord, Webhooks, or GitHub Issues.

### Team Collaboration
Invite your team, assign roles, and manage incidents together.
