# Teams

Teams let you organize monitors, collaborate on incidents, and manage access for your organization.

## How Teams Work

- Every monitor, incident, and heartbeat belongs to a team
- Team members can view and manage team resources
- Each member has a role that determines their permissions
- You can be a member of multiple teams

## Personal vs Organization Teams

### Personal Team

When you sign up, a personal team is created automatically. Use this for:
- Personal projects
- Testing and experimentation
- Solo work

### Organization Teams

Create organization teams to:
- Collaborate with colleagues
- Share monitors across a group
- Manage incidents together
- Control access with roles

## Creating a Team

1. Click on the team selector in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Team**
3. Enter a team name
4. Click **Create**

You're automatically the owner of teams you create.

## Team Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access, can delete team, manage billing |
| **Admin** | Manage team settings, add/remove members |
| **Member** | View and manage monitors, incidents, heartbeats |

### Owner

- All admin permissions
- Delete the team
- Transfer ownership
- Manage billing (if applicable)

### Admin

- All member permissions
- Invite and remove members
- Change member roles
- Update team settings

### Member

- View all team resources
- Create and manage monitors
- Create and manage heartbeats
- Manage incidents
- Configure notifications

## Inviting Team Members

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter their email address
4. Select a role
5. Click **Send Invite**

The invited person receives an email with a link to join your team.

### Pending Invites

View and manage pending invitations:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. See the **Pending Invites** section
3. Resend or cancel invites as needed

## Managing Members

### Changing Roles

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Find the member
3. Click on their role
4. Select the new role

### Removing Members

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Find the member
3. Click **Remove**
4. Confirm

Removed members lose access immediately.

## Switching Teams

If you're a member of multiple teams:

1. Click the team selector in the sidebar
2. Select the team you want to work in

The dashboard shows only that team's monitors, incidents, and data.

## Leaving a Team

To leave a team you're a member of:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Click **Leave Team**
3. Confirm

You can't leave if you're the only owner. Transfer ownership first.

## Transferring Ownership

To transfer team ownership:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Find the member who will become owner
3. Click **Transfer Ownership**
4. Confirm

You become an admin after transferring ownership.

## Deleting a Team

Only owners can delete teams.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
3. Click **Delete Team**
4. Type the team name to confirm
5. Click **Delete**

Deleting a team removes all its data including monitors, incidents, and history. This cannot be undone.

## Best Practices

1. **Use descriptive team names** - "Backend Team" vs "Team 1"
2. **Limit owners** - Only give owner access when necessary
3. **Use admin for managers** - They can manage members without full control
4. **Regular audits** - Review member access periodically
5. **Remove promptly** - Remove access when people leave

## Common Questions

### Can I be in multiple teams?

Yes. Switch between teams using the team selector.

### Can monitors be shared between teams?

No. Each monitor belongs to exactly one team. If multiple teams need to monitor the same service, create separate monitors.

### What happens to my data if I leave a team?

Team data stays with the team. You lose access but nothing is deleted.

### Can I merge teams?

Not directly. You would need to recreate monitors and move data manually.
